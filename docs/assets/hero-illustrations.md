# Hero illustrations

There are 68 hero illustrations, each a 320 × 160 (2:1) SVG with a transparent background. They're scenes of people and objects that convey a complex idea or a feeling, used mostly in onboarding flows and overlays.

## Using them

- In code: `<Anvil.Illustration type="hero" name="anytime" background="purple-subdued" />`. `Anvil.Illustration.names.hero` lists them all.
- In a Card, pass the file as the hero image.
- Use one hero per screen and let it fill the width. Don't crop people out of the scene, and don't recolor.

## Groups

- **Programs:** programs-diabetes, programs-hypertension, programs-weight-management, programs-mental-health, programs-healthy-living, programs-chronic-kidney-disease
- **Devices and setup:** device-* (connected device pairs), and the step-by-step set for blood pressure monitors (-ht2000) and scales (-wm2000)

- **Tasks and progress:** plan, task, checked-off, progress, progress-5-steps, scheduling, reminder, visit
- **Coaching:** coach-1, coach-2, coach-3, relationships, parent-education
